# Project Specification

## Core setup
Strong Liquidity → Strong Supply/Demand Zone → Strong Price Action → FVG/IFVG/Imbalance → BOS → Retest → Confirmation → HTF Alignment → Risk Check → Trade Plan

## Timeframes
1D, 4H, 1H, 15M, 5M

## Markets
Top 70 Crypto, Top 20 Forex, Top 5 Futures

## Trade plan
Long/Short, Entry, SL, TP, Break-even, trade history and risk management.

## UI
Responsive desktop/mobile foundation with scanner, chart area and SMC logic display.

## Initial safety
Trade-plan generation only. Real-money execution is disabled until the signal engine is validated.
